## What is confirmed about counters in First Person Boxing?

The official game description names counters as a core mechanic and describes them as a major part of the first-person boxing experience. It does not publish a counter multiplier, recovery window, stun duration, or list of attacks that qualify. Those details must be tested in the current build before they become factual guide content.

A counter should therefore be discussed as a decision to verify, not as a guaranteed combo copied from another fighting game.

## What can create a possible counter opening?

A defended or missed attack may leave an opponent in a position where a response is possible, but the opening still depends on what is visible in game. Check the remaining distance, stamina state, opponent animation, and your own recovery before committing. The public source does not promise that every successful guard or slip grants a free hit.

Use this observation table during AI practice:

| Observation | Question before responding |
| --- | --- |
| Opponent’s punch missed | Has the attack animation actually finished? |
| Guard absorbed an attack | Is another attack already starting? |
| Slip changed your position | Are you still in range to respond? |
| Stamina is low | Can you act without triggering visible fatigue? |
| Opponent remains centered | Can you see enough to judge the next action? |

The [guards and slips guide](/combat/guards-and-slips/) covers how to isolate the defensive part of this test.

## How should you practice counters without inventing timing?

Begin against AI with one recognizable attack. Defend or avoid it, attempt a single response at a visible point in the animation, and record whether it connects. Repeat the same scenario before moving the response earlier or later. A reproducible sequence is stronger evidence than a phrase such as “counter during recovery.”

If the game later exposes a counter indicator, damage number, or training log, capture it with the date and version context. Until then, do not claim exact frames or bonus damage.

## What do we know about knockdowns and replays?

The official description confirms **cinematic knockdowns and replays**. It does not explain whether knockdowns depend on health, stamina, accumulated damage, a specific punch, or another hidden condition. It also does not document the difference between a knockdown and a final knockout.

Test these events separately and record the conditions visible before each result. The [combat basics guide](/combat/combat-basics/) lists the other variables to watch, while the [stamina guide](/combat/stamina/) prevents resource state from being ignored.

## Which knockout claims remain unverified?

Counter damage bonuses, stun rules, knockdown thresholds, standing counts, round limits, replay triggers, knockout victory conditions, and any “best finishing punch” remain unsupported by the reviewed official sources. They need developer documentation or controlled player tests.
