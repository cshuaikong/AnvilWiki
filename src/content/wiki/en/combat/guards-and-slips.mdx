## What is the difference between guards and slips?

First Person Boxing officially describes different guards for protecting the head and body, while slips are used to avoid punches. A guard responds to the threatened target area; a slip attempts to move away from the attack line. The public listing does not disclose reduction values, stamina costs, directions, or timing frames.

That means the most useful starting point is learning what each input and animation does in the current build.

## How do head and body guards work?

The official source confirms the two protected areas but stops there. Verify the current inputs and visual states in AI practice, then compare what happens when the same visible attack meets the matching guard and the nonmatching guard. Avoid assigning percentages or “always safe” labels without repeatable evidence.

Record these observations:

| Head guard selected | Stance/UI change and result against a head-targeting attack |
| Body guard selected | Stance/UI change and result against a body-targeting attack |

| Guard switched | Delay, animation, and whether the new area is protected |

## How should you test slips?

Use AI practice and begin with a single readable attack. Confirm the slip input, observe the movement direction and animation, then note whether the punch connects. Repeat from a similar distance before changing the timing. This method measures the current game instead of assuming the slip works like another Roblox title.

Third-party advice often says to slip at the “last safe moment,” but no sourced frame window is available. Treat that wording as a practice idea rather than a measured rule.

## Can a defensive action create a counter opportunity?

The game officially features counters, so defense and retaliation are related parts of the combat design. However, the public description does not define a guaranteed counter window after every guard or slip. Distance, stamina, the opponent’s continued action, and the current build can all affect what follows.

Learn the decision in the [counters and knockouts guide](/combat/counters-and-knockouts/), and review the [stamina guide](/combat/stamina/) before assuming every defensive action should immediately become an attack.

## What defense claims still need player evidence?

Guard reduction, chip damage, stamina drain, guard-break rules, switch delay, slip directions, invulnerability frames, recovery time, counter bonus, and matchup-specific behavior all remain unverified. A screenshot, dated recording, or developer statement is needed before publishing those details as fact.
