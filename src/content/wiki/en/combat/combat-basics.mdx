## What are the First Person Boxing combat basics?

First Person Boxing combat combines first-person positioning with punches, slips, separate head and body guards, stamina management, counters, and knockdowns. These systems are named in the official Roblox description. Start by recognizing their on-screen feedback in AI practice rather than memorizing damage values or timing windows that have not been verified.

The [beginner guide](/guides/getting-started/) provides the full first-session path. If you still need to identify the inputs on your device, begin with the [controls guide](/controls/controls-basics/).

## How do the combat systems connect?

Each decision changes what options remain in the exchange. Position and camera determine what you can read. A guard protects a target area, while a slip attempts to avoid the incoming punch. Stamina limits how freely actions can continue, and a successful defensive read may create a counter opportunity.

## How should you build a basic practice loop?

Use a short observation loop against AI: establish distance, wait for a readable action, choose one defensive response, and observe whether an attack is safe afterward. Reset your position and stamina before repeating the same test. This is a practice method, not a claim that the game enforces a fixed five-step combo.

Change only one variable per attempt. For example, compare head guard and body guard against the same visible threat, or compare a guard with a slip while keeping the starting position similar. Controlled tests produce more useful guidance than a highlight clip.

## Why do guards and slips need separate practice?

The official description treats guards and slips as different defensive tools. Head and body guards protect target areas, while a slip changes how the incoming punch connects. Their exact advantages cannot be ranked without current gameplay evidence, so first learn what the UI and animations communicate.

Use the [guards and slips guide](/combat/guards-and-slips/) for a repeatable test plan. The [POV guide](/guides/first-person-boxing-pov/) helps with keeping the relevant animation visible.

## How should stamina change your decisions?

Stamina is a resource because the official description warns that fatigue slows you down. Watch the meter while performing repeated actions, stop before assuming a fixed threshold, and compare the same sequence across several attempts. The public source does not say that every action costs the same amount—or even publish a cost table.

Continue with the [stamina guide](/combat/stamina/) for a focused testing method. When you can recognize a defensive result without losing track of the meter, the [counter and knockout guide](/combat/counters-and-knockouts/) explains how to evaluate the next decision.

## Which combat claims still need evidence?

Exact punch lists, damage, reach, active and recovery frames, guard reduction, stamina cost, counter multipliers, stun duration, and knockout thresholds all need current in-game evidence or a developer statement. Until that exists, this wiki will describe observable systems and testing steps instead of presenting invented numbers as a complete combat formula.
