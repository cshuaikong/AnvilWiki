## Where should you start?

Use this section to find a guide for the encounter you are preparing
for. Start with its requirements and preparation checklist, then read
the attack patterns and recommended strategy.

If you have already attempted the fight, focus on the mechanic that
ended your last run rather than repeating the entire preparation process.

## What should you check before an encounter?

- **Requirements:** Check whether the encounter needs a specific
  unlock, location, or progression milestone.
- **Preparation:** Review the equipment and resources listed in the
  individual guide.
- **Attack patterns:** Identify warning cues and opportunities to act.
- **Rewards:** Check the guide's sources and verification date before
  planning around a particular reward.

## How should you use these guides?

Read the quick answer first for an overview. Use the detailed sections
to understand the steps, and consult screenshots or videos when a
mechanic is difficult to describe in words.

Game updates can change encounter behavior. Each guide's verification
date helps you judge whether its information matches the current game.
